Insurance and Financial Law

For this course only the syllabus is available.

Syllabus

  • Basic characteristics of the Hungarian legal system.
  • Legal rules governing the insurance sector.
  • Insurance contract law: the Civil Code and the Motor Vehicle Liability Insurance Act.
  • Civil Code and Motor Vehicle Liability Insurance Act II.
  • Public law regulation of insurance activity: structure, scope and definitions of the Insurance Act.
  • Participants in the insurance market: insurers and intermediaries.
  • Establishing an insurance company and starting insurance activity: the most important operating conditions.
  • The most important operating conditions of insurance companies II.
  • The most important operating conditions of insurance companies III.
  • Overview of relevant capital market regulation.
  • The role of the Hungarian National Bank as supervisory authority in capital markets.
  • The connection between securities law and insurance activity.
  • Introduction to the concept of securities: distinguishing securities from contracts and from objects.
  • Rules on the forms, issuance and cancellation of securities.
  • Overview of the regulation of basic securities.
  • Overview of capital market participants.
  • Supervision of the financial sector: tasks and instruments of the Hungarian National Bank.
