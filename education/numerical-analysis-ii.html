Numerical Analysis II

For this course only the syllabus is available.

Syllabus

  • Numerical integration: elementary and composite quadrature formulas.
  • Orthogonal polynomials and Gaussian quadrature methods.
  • Numerical treatment of special integrands.
  • Initial value problems for ordinary differential equations: explicit and implicit Euler methods.
  • Consistency, stability, and convergence of numerical methods for ODEs.
  • Inheritance of asymptotic stability under numerical discretisation.
  • Explicit Runge–Kutta methods for initial value problems.
  • Linear multistep methods: order of the method, root condition, and stability.
  • Finite difference discretisation of basic elliptic and parabolic partial differential equations on equidistant grids.
  • Solution techniques based on Fourier methods.
  • The Fast Fourier Transform algorithm and its numerical role.
