Operations Research Methods

For this course only the syllabus is available.

Syllabus

  • Linear programming fundamentals and the geometry of feasible solution sets.
  • The simplex method and its interpretation in solving linear programming problems.
  • The dual simplex method and its applications.
  • Farkas' lemma, linear programming duality and the strong duality theorem.
  • The connection between absence of arbitrage and risk-neutral measures, with proof based on strong duality.
  • Mixed-integer linear programming and the branch-and-bound method.
  • Cutting-plane methods for integer and mixed-integer optimization.
  • Bank cash optimization as an applied operations research problem.
  • Portfolio optimization and financial applications of mathematical programming.
  • Data analysis problems formulated as linear and mixed-integer programming models: regression, clustering and classification.
